Last updated: July 2026

By creating a Zehnify account, users agree to the terms displayed at signup and at `/terms` in the frontend application.

Key points:
- UMT @umt.edu.pk email required
- Service is wellness support, not emergency care
- Camera mood data is reviewed by clinical staff
- New users register as patients; doctor role is admin-assigned
- Signup requires `acceptedTerms: true` (stored as `acceptedTermsAt` on the user record)
